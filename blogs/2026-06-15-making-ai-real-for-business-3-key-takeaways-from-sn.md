Drawing on themes from Snowflake Summit 2026, Clint Richardson argues that AI agents and agentic workflows are the most effective way to make AI practical, but warns that without trustworthy data these systems can rapidly spread inaccurate insights. He positions reliable data mastering, golden records, and contextual data about entity relationships as foundational to operationalizing AI in business.
