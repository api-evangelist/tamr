This article examines whether organizations should build custom master data management (MDM) solutions or purchase established platforms like Tamr. It identifies five key considerations -- scalability, entity resolution complexity, security, hidden costs, and skills gaps -- that favor buying over building, arguing companies should focus engineering effort on business-specific workflows that sit on top of trusted master data rather than recreating core MDM functionality.
